## Customer journey / outcome matrix

Four phases, left to right: Consideration, Purchase, Delivery, Returns &
Exchanges. Consideration and Purchase are "Pre-Purchase"; Delivery and
Returns & Exchanges are "Post-Purchase."

| Phase | Product | Retailer capability | Business outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consideration | Convert | Accurate and attractive Estimated Delivery Date | Increase Web Conversion Rate |
| Purchase | Convert | Checkout-stage delivery promise | Decrease WISMO Inquiries |
| Delivery | Engage | Embedded tracking; branded & personalised comms; reporting | Increase Web Traffic; Increase Customer Touchpoints; Improve Repurchase Rate; Decrease WISMO Inquiries |
| Returns & Exchanges | Retain | Embedded Returns Portal; branded & personalised comms | Reduce Returns; Gain Transparency; Reduce WISMR Inquiries |

Confirmed 2026-04-13 (`parcelLab at a Glance.pptx`) and consistent with every
other real deck sampled this session (Cybex Overview, Frilufts RFP, G-III,
Bike24, and the Bike24 Kickoff deck, which reframes the same matrix as "why
we're here").
